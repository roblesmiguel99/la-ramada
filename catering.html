Fired hot, delivered to your event.

La Ramada caters weddings, quinceañeras, birthdays, and work events around Milton-Freewater — the same food from the same kitchen, delivered to your location. No fixed packages, no online form: call and we'll work out what your event needs.

What catering includes

Off-site delivery We bring the food to your event — no need to pick anything up.
The real kitchen Catering food is made in the same kitchen, from the same recipes, as what's served in the dining room.
Built from Family Dinners Camarones, enchiladas, tacos, and Cielo Mar y Tierra are the base — larger or custom orders by request.
Drop-off, not staffed We deliver the food; we don't provide serving staff or equipment setup at your event.
Call ahead There's no fixed minimum or online form — call and we'll talk through timing, quantities, and your event.

Occasions we cater

Any gathering where people need to eat well. A few we cater often:

  • Weddings
  • Quinceañeras
  • Birthdays & celebrations
  • Work & community events
