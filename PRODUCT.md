# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
delegated: static HTML/CSS. No online ordering, accounts, or reservation system is evidenced — this is an informational/marketing site (menu, hours, location, contact), so a static build is simplest to make and host and avoids unneeded framework overhead.

## Users
Local diners in and around Milton-Freewater, OR deciding where to eat (dine-in or to-go), plus people planning a family gathering, birthday party, or celebration who need catering or a family dinner package.

## Product Purpose
Marketing/informational site for La Ramada Mexican Family Restaurant. Primary job: let a visitor browse the menu and decide to visit or order. Secondary job: surface location, hours, and phone number so a same-day decision is easy, and make the family dinner / catering / celebration offerings visible as a real revenue line, not an afterthought.

## Positioning
A Mexican family restaurant, not a bar or upscale/trendy concept — the differentiator is a warm, friendly, family-style dining environment ("friendly environment full of friendly people and beautiful Mexican dishes"), reinforced by family dinner packages sized for groups and celebrations (birthdays, catering).

## Operating Context
Single physical location, dine-in restaurant with to-go orders. Offers family dinner packages, catering, and hosts birthday parties/celebrations. Small-town local business (Milton-Freewater, OR) — visitors are often deciding same-day.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- No online ordering or reservation system evidenced on the incumbent site — treat as out of scope unless the user asks for it.
- Hours vary by day (see Evidence on Hand); must display accurately, not as a single blanket hours line.
- Single location — no multi-location logic needed.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: "La Ramada Mexican Family Restaurant" (short form: "La Ramada").
- Existing tagline: "Join us and enjoy your dinner in a friendly environment full of friendly people and beautiful Mexican dishes."
- Voice: warm, welcoming, family-oriented — not upscale or trendy.
- Existing social presence: Facebook (@LaRamadaMF); the incumbent site also lists Twitter, Google+, LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube, but several of those platforms are defunct or likely stale — verify before linking rather than carrying them forward uncritically.

## Product Principles
1. Menu-first: the shortest path for a visitor is to the menu — this was confirmed as the primary desired action.
2. Practical info (location, hours, phone) must be immediately findable, not buried — visitors are often deciding same-day.
3. Warmth and hospitality are the message, not visual trendiness — family-run character over polish-for-its-own-sake.
4. Group/celebration offerings (family dinners, catering, birthdays) are a real business line and should be visible, not hidden.
5. Never invent menu items, prices, hours, or claims beyond what's confirmed here — flag gaps instead of filling them.
